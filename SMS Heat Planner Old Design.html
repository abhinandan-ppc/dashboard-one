Routing Optimization Engine
  • Interactive Flowchart: Scroll down and click any card in the flowchart to instantly adjust its specific equipment settings, targets, or speeds.
  • Priority: Mills are fed strictly by EBITDA ₹/tonne ranking (Greedy Algorithm). High-paying mills completely lock down required time slots before low-paying mills evaluate availability.
  • Distance Affinity Routing: The engine uses Asymmetric Gap Mapping to enforce strict equipment fallback sequences natively based on bay proximity.
  • Cross-Bay Logistical Penalty: Inter-bay crossovers are heavily penalized mathematically compared to intra-bay routing. The engine only pulls across the shop if local equipment fails.
